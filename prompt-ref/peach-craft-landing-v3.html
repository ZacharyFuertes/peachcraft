Our Story

Sculpted alone, one order at a time, in a small home studio in Quezon City.

Peach Craft started in 2021 as a way to make something that lasts — kawaii clay pieces and fake cakes shaped entirely by hand, no molds, no shortcuts. Every order still passes through one maker, start to finish.
